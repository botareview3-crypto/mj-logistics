About MJ Logistics

The same verification desk, three stops along the route

MJ Logistics began as a wholesale auto parts marketplace based in Monrovia, matching buyers to factory-verified suppliers. That same escrow-backed verification desk now covers gold and diamond sourcing — one route, one standard of proof, across every trade.

How a trade moves

Three steps, whether it's a crate or a parcel

01 — POST

Post an RFQ

Specify the part and fitment, or the metal, purity and volume. Verified suppliers see it within the hour.

02 — BID

Suppliers bid your spec

Compare price, lead time and verification tier side by side — no blind sourcing.

03 — CLEAR

Funds clear through escrow

Payment releases once the shipment or parcel is confirmed against the documented spec.

Verification

What "verified" means here

Every supplier on either side of the route clears the same desk before a listing goes live.

Identity

Business registration and beneficial ownership confirmed before onboarding.

Origin

Mineral lots carry documented origin and handling history through export.

Escrow

Payment is held, not routed, until the buyer confirms the shipment.

Compliance

Kimberley Process and customs paperwork prepared alongside every mineral trade.
